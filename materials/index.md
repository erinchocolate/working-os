# Materials Index

This is the personal reusable expression-materials library for distilled ideas
that can travel to future projects, roles, deep sharing, learning, and
reflection.

Notion remains the source of truth for raw notes. `worklog/`, `reviews/`, and
`sharing/` keep process material locally. This directory stores selected
Zettelkasten-style material cards only: concise, reusable, source-linked, and
written mainly in the owner's own words.

`assets/mental-models.md` remains the home for a small number of validated
action principles that should change how the owner works. `materials/` is
different: it stores ideas, stories, analogies, frameworks, and questions that
can help explain something to other people.

## Rules

- Use Chinese by default for material cards, unless a source term needs to stay
  in English.
- Keep source links so the original Notion note, article, or chat can be
  revisited.
- Keep origin links when a card comes from `worklog/`, `reviews/recurring.md`,
  `assets/mental-models.md`, or a learning note.
- Do not copy long passages from articles or other people's work. Use short
  quotes only when useful, and summarize the main idea in the owner's own words.
- Do not store sensitive company content, PII, credentials, private raw meeting
  notes, or long copyrighted excerpts.
- Route shared project/company facts to `/opt/processes/context_platform`, not
  to `materials/`.
- Only keep material that still has value after the original task/project ends.
